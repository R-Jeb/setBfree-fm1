CC 65 switches it on, CC 93 sets the drive (0 to 16, as KNOB 3 on the FM-1): setBfree's overdrive.inputgain in steps of 1.5 dB, 12 is its default. The output follows the drive, so on and off are about equally loud.

The character is setBfree's overdrive.character (0 to 127, 16 in firmware 0.4): it sets the two bass filters around the tube curve. It goes as NRPN 1/0 (CC 99 = 1, CC 98 = 0, CC 6 = value) and needs firmware 0.5. The output is matched to character 16: other values can be louder or quieter (in setBfree too: about −17 dB at 32, +6 dB at 127); the limiter keeps the peaks in.

Leslie

Speed

CC 1 selects slow, stop or fast; CC 64 (sustain pedal) toggles slow and fast.

setBfree's whirl.horn/drum.slowrpm, fastrpm, acceleration and deceleration (the time to get about two thirds of the way to the new speed). They go as NRPN 2/0 to 2/7 with 14 bits (CC 99 = 2, CC 98 = 0..7, CC 6 and CC 38: 0.1 rpm, or ms) and need firmware 0.5. The FM-1 keeps them with its settings, for all programs.

Program

Program change

setBfree's default.pgm: 110 programs, and your own ones at 112 to 127 (firmware 0.5). The FM-1 takes them as program change n − 1. A setBfree program changes only what it names; your own ones set everything they hold.

Settings as text

MIDI monitor

Orange: sent by the editor. Green: arrives from the FM-1 or a keyboard on the selected input. With firmware 0.5 the editor reads the FM-1's sound when you connect and when you press Read from FM-1 (SysEx); with an older firmware it shows what it last sent.

UpperLowerPedal

The firmware plays channel 1 on the upper manual, 2 on the lower, 3 on the pedal. Change this only if you changed it in the firmware.
