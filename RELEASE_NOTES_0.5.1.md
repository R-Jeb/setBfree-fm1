# B3 ORGAN 0.5.1

A logo, an install page and two small fixes; the organ sounds as in 0.5. What is new since 0.5:

- **A logo** after switching on: a row of drawbars over the name. It comes back as a screen saver after a minute without a knob, a
  button or a key. The first knob or button only brings back the upper manual's page (it changes nothing); a key plays at once.
- **The install shows its progress on the FM-1:** the logo with a bar under it while it waits (UPDATE MODE), while it writes
  (WRITING 57 %, DO NOT SWITCH OFF) and if it fails (UPDATE FAILED, INSTALL AGAIN). After 100 % the FM-1 restarts.
- **Install from the browser:** [r-jeb.github.io/setBfree-fm1](https://r-jeb.github.io/setBfree-fm1/) in Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 on USB,
  with the four pages and every control explained. It also takes the FM-1 back to the official firmware.
- The **lower and pedal pages** show the footage numbers under their drawbars again (16, 5 1/3, 8 ...); they were cut off on the FM-1.
- The **pedal page** no longer shows the load: page 4 (test values) has it, with the MIDI messages.
- A first start without saved settings has setBfree's **drive 12** (it was 3, from the old character steps).

Your settings and your own programs 112 to 127 stay through the update.

Install: on the page above, or `b3organ-0.5.1.fwsc` with the FM-1 Firmware Hub or the README's other ways. 0.5 stays available under Releases.
Please report what sounds different from setBfree.
